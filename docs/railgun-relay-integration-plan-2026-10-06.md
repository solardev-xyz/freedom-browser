# Railgun gas relaying: implementation continuation

Railgun's restricted Kohaku adapters and authenticated recovery companion are implemented and have controlled native evidence. Their current private broadcaster submits through the wallet's enrolled Ethereum account. Gas relaying is the next distinct integration: a broadcaster receives a private fee and submits the transaction from its own Ethereum account.

The current recovery milestone is documented in [authenticated recovery](railgun-recovery-companion-2026-10-06.md). This plan does not extend its qualification to relaying. The fee/proof experiment and Electron entrypoint correction are committed through `0cc74e6c`; 168 focused tests, strict lint and changed-file formatting pass. The first native attempt never entered the experiment because the Electron app loader did not satisfy its Node entrypoint guard. That attempt was stopped, retained as a failure, and produced no proof report. The corrected fresh run completed naturally with both minimum-gas cases verified independently, 13 altered-public-signal refusals per case, and four intentional utility closures. Source/runtime/artifact checks remained unchanged. This establishes the synthetic fee/pre-transaction proof shape only; deployed service acceptance remains untested.

The [offline wire experiment](railgun-relay-wire-2026-10-06.md) now passes 39 checks against selected upstream functions. Its genuine test-key registry, dummy calldata and excluded module initialization limit this evidence: arbitrary peer admission, actual fee/proof composition and transport remain open. The [repository reproduction recipe](qualification/railgun-relay-wire-recipe-2026-10-06/OVERVIEW.md) now passes a separate fresh 39-case replay at `258bbee2`, with 114 focused tests and strict lint. That checkpoint does not itself carry real transaction proofs; the later composition below covers the join.

The [key-admission candidate](railgun-relay-key-admission-2026-10-06.md) separately passes 17 offline checks. It replaces a fixed fixture-key allowlist with canonical prime-subgroup point validation and a documented stricter R/S policy. Production integration, operator trust and wire binding remain open; the candidate deliberately excludes a mathematically valid zero-nonce signature. A [fresh repository replay](qualification/railgun-relay-keys-recipe-2026-10-06/OVERVIEW.md) at `6bc9456e` reproduces all seventeen cases and exact call counts; 133 affected tests and strict lint pass.

The [actual proof-to-wire composition](qualification/railgun-relay-proof-wire-2026-10-06/OVERVIEW.md) now passes one native run at `41472ed4`. Four guarded utilities connect pre-producer synthetic quote/fee admission, real transaction/pre-transaction POI proofs, selected upstream COMMON encryption and separate verification of reconstructed decrypted data. Exact public proof/calldata and signed-quote bytes are retained. This closes the local composition prerequisite; genuine operation authority, deployed service acceptance and production submission remain unqualified. A standalone public cryptographic reverifier is being reviewed separately.

The [single-attempt handoff model](railgun-relay-handoff-model-2026-10-06.md) at `49cf9e5e` passes 30 unit tests with fake authority, keys, journal and transport; eleven detached mutants distinguish the intended ordering and ownership. It captures write-before-send uncertainty, original promises and callback drainage without implementing authenticated storage or a production permit. Next are genuine fee/operation admission and durable attempt binding, followed by confined transport and specifically authorized service qualification.

## Upstream sources and compatibility

The official [broadcaster guide](https://docs.railgun.org/developer-guide/wallet/broadcasters) identifies the [client](https://github.com/Railgun-Community/waku-broadcaster-client/tree/ef8d2b68720f6b51dd411e94eaf367d473844391). Client 9.1.1 declares wallet ^10.9.0 and shared-models ^8.0.1 peers, matching our pinned wallet 10.9.0 / engine 9.6.0 generation. Its supported broadcaster-service version range, 8.0.0–8.999.0, is a different version domain. No source-indicated engine downgrade is needed. This establishes declared compatibility, not an installed build or deployed-service test.

The [server example](https://github.com/Railgun-Community/ppoi-safe-broadcaster-example/tree/ec49691533b4edc2dafefbd8cdb5f611290b2115) supplies the other side of the protocol. Both repositories were inspected without installing dependencies or contacting live peers. They resolve the earlier missing-source question; live Sepolia availability, supported lists and deployed versions remain unverified.

A signed fee advertisement authenticates its exact bytes to the public viewing key decoded from the advertised Railgun address. It does not independently establish a trusted operator or price. The signed object has no explicit chain type/id. A chain-specific Railgun address contributes a signed chain restriction; an all-chains address does not. The Waku topic supplies additional local chain context and is not part of the signed object. Our local admission must bind the selected chain, deployment, broadcaster address/key, fee and operation without claiming a stronger upstream signature domain. Address-encoded chain data must be locally checked and supplies no deployment binding. A Waku discovery, Store or LightPush peer is a transport intermediary, separate from the broadcaster identified by the signed address/key and fee ID; both identities and disclosure destinations need explicit handling.

Requests use ephemeral shared-key encryption and include the broadcaster's public viewing key, chain, fee ID, calldata and pre-transaction POIs. That field is not the user's viewing secret. Replies use the same shared key. A decryptable reply establishes neither canonical inclusion nor finality; errors or timeouts can occur after submission. The upstream client's returned hash can also come from nullifier lookup rather than a broadcaster reply.

The inspected server is a reference implementation, not an independent guard we can rely on. Its topic/payload chain mismatch check logs rather than rejects, its fee cache can recognize expired IDs, and it records ephemeral request keys before decryption while ignoring duplicates. That bounded duplicate history is neither durable exactly-once execution nor a response replay mechanism. Quote expiry, cancellation and an unchanged or replaced exchange key cannot establish remote nonexecution.

## Work sequence

1. **Fee and pre-transaction proof feasibility.** Qualify a disposable one-input transaction with a 100-unit broadcaster fee first and a 900-unit self output second, consuming a synthetic 1000-unit WETH note. Use the existing 01x02 transaction and POI_3x3 artifacts, and verify in a separate process. Check minimum gas price zero and one with separate signatures, and distinguish old-signature/public-signal failures from output-annotation checks. The pre-transaction POI uses a separate domain from mined own-operation POI. Existing production policy must keep refusing this new shape until its authority and storage design is reviewed. This experiment qualifies neither relayed full nor partial unshield. Fee plus partial unshield/change requires another output shape and connected reconstruction, receipt, POI and subsequent-spend qualification.
2. **Offline protocol compatibility.** Exercise exact upstream signed advertisement bytes, address-derived keys, request encryption and reply decryption against disposable keys. Add strict bounds, expiry checks, request ownership and cross-operation substitution controls. Require signature verification even under the upstream development/no-crypto.subtle bypass conditions; missing or invalid signatures must never qualify an advertisement. Preserve wire compatibility while keeping per-operation state. No discovery, network transport, authentic service quote or spending authority follows from an offline vector.
3. **Production admission and durable uncertainty.** Introduce an explicit versioned relay intent binding reviewed fee token/rate, gas estimate and buffer, maximum authorized fee, net value, recipient, minimum gas, broadcaster identity and exact proof. A fixed 100-unit synthetic fee does not demonstrate sufficient service payment. Recheck quote lifetime before emission; if it expires during proving, refuse and require an explicitly reviewed rebuild before admission. Never silently reprice or rebuild an admitted uncertain attempt. First settle the transport ownership contract: the precise handoff boundary, per-operation exchange-key retention, late responses and crash recovery must inform durable admission. Review must precede selected-input POI/root queries and eventual relay disclosure, not merely the final send. Create a separate pre-transaction POI authority and a durable relay-attempt record before transport handoff. Do not fabricate local EOA signatures, nonces or hashes for remote submission. A lost reply retains the hold; quote expiry is not evidence that the signed transaction is unusable. Recover the original attempt without automatic retries or peer switching, and reconcile against independently checked chain calldata, receipts and finality.
4. **Confined transport and live qualification.** Design the Waku discovery, Filter, Store and LightPush runtime around account separation, reviewed destinations, cancellation and observable shutdown. The Node client uses direct libp2p TCP/DNS; the web variant has WebSocket transport paths, but neither is evidence of Tor routing. Do not replace the actual protocol with an invented HTTP endpoint or silently fall back to direct networking. Dependency installation and deployed-service qualification remain separate review steps.

The existing Kohaku surface stays the integration boundary. Fee/proof, authority, persistence and transport belong in their current wallet/network responsibilities; the adapter can delegate a genuine reviewed operation to the appropriate submitter. Portable Host support and eventual extraction into a separate reusable repository remain distinct from granting Freedom's internal account authority.

## Behaviors to preserve

Keep original promises and durable outcomes, fresh proof/preflight authority, one-use admission, prior-attempt protection and canonical reconciliation. The upstream client has a static response slot and automatically rebroadcasts through nominal second 20, with a nominal total timeout of 120 seconds; it has no per-operation AbortSignal. Those behaviors cannot become our recovery policy by importing the client wholesale.

The funded owned-note/service disclosure gate remains unresolved. A relay request would add its own disclosure of decrypted transaction/proof/fee data to the chosen broadcaster; Waku intermediaries receive the encrypted envelope and associated transport metadata. Neither this research nor disposable proof tests authorize those disclosures, live private spending, a return-to-origin exception, production activation or product UI. The PPv2 live relay journey remains the comparison target; Railgun has not yet reached that live milestone.
