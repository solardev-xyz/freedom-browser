# Offline broadcaster key admission — 6 October 2026

The reviewed key-admission experiment passed all 17 declared cases: three positive, six refusal, six distinguishing/snapshot controls and two limitations. The original process exited naturally with code 0 in 0.090 seconds. It made 10 signature-verification calls, six guarded ECDH calls and four actual wallet encryptions. Independent review confirmed the report, exact case set, source/runtime joins and counters without rerunning crypto. [Exact report](https://github.com/solardev-xyz/railgun-kohaku-adapter/blob/377c2a5d1aca18f0ed328dfdda955bd7d1ba271c/docs/freedom-qualification/railgun-relay-key-admission-2026-10-06/report.json) · [Provenance](https://github.com/solardev-xyz/railgun-kohaku-adapter/blob/377c2a5d1aca18f0ed328dfdda955bd7d1ba271c/docs/freedom-qualification/railgun-relay-key-admission-2026-10-06/provenance.json).

The candidate replaces the previous experiment's fixed synthetic-key allowlist with explicit mathematical admission: a public point must decode strictly, round-trip to its original bytes, be nonidentity, not have small order and be torsion-free. Three independently Node-derived genuine keys passed, including a third key outside the earlier registry. All eight pinned Noble torsion encodings were refused. A mixed-torsion point demonstrated why canonical encoding and a small-order check alone are insufficient.

Signature R and scalar S have separate checks. The local hardened R policy deliberately rejects identity. A constructed zero-nonce signature with identity R passed both original Node (v24.18.1 / OpenSSL 3.5.7) and Noble verification but was refused by this local policy. **That signature was mathematically valid**; the refusal documents an intentional compatibility restriction, not a forgery or a claim of RFC/ZIP conformance. Original genuine Node/Noble signatures remained interoperable.

Actual wallet encryption and server-delegate decryption exercised the broadcaster recipient and client ephemeral-key roles. Caller mutation across real signature, ECDH and wallet-helper awaits could not change the detached inputs. Returned byte copies could not alter retained admission tokens. Invalid-peer ECDH checks asserted rejection and unchanged helper-call counts; those particular tests did not independently attribute every rejection to a specific guard code.

This qualifies a bounded offline candidate, not production relay authority. Mathematical key validity does not identify an operator, authenticate a Waku peer, sign an external topic, validate a quote price or establish transaction finality. No off-curve adversarial vector, independent curve implementation, provider, funded profile, native/prover worker or live relay was exercised. The existing [relay-wire experiment](railgun-relay-wire-2026-10-06.md) and [integration plan](railgun-relay-integration-plan-2026-10-06.md) retain the broader transport, quote and recovery limits.

## Preservation and next use

The report is copied byte-for-byte. Provenance is a derived public index: it retains original hashes, observed exit, reviewer scope and package-relative source identifiers while omitting machine-local paths. The exact source/policy/vector archives remain identified by their immutable hashes. This compact evidence is not a self-contained executable package or a portable dependency-resolution promise.

A later repository fixture can preserve the reviewed gate and cases with exact Noble bindings and source pins. Production integration still needs separate trust/discovery policy, quote/fee/proof binding and durable transport admission. This result alone authorizes none of those actions. Any replay should stage the original frozen candidate into a fresh reviewed environment and preserve the original report; new execution is separate evidence.
