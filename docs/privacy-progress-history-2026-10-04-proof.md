**Controlled local proof:** [The new main-only controller](https://github.com/solardev-xyz/freedom-browser/blob/e211e8e69fce7aa8f7d285e4a729d4e66b31b588/docs/railgun-own-poi-proof-2026-10-04.md) takes preparation only from a genuine post-spend Shield membership receipt. Its supported scope is one WETH Shield input with self-transfer or full unshield. A fresh authenticated recovery window compares the operation, reattests before viewing-key derivation and again after derivation, and repeats identity/generation/deadline checks immediately before copying the key. A valid purpose-and-input-bound request consumes one attempt before awaiting anything. Earlier refusals leave the receipt reusable. No spending private key is requested.

The isolated job authenticates runtime/artifacts before its key request, reconstructs private data internally and proves once. Main derives the required-list root, TXID root/checkpoint index, marker and output count, reconstructs the payload and checks its digest. Host key copies are wiped. Utility exit and pending broker work must drain before recovery releases; a permanently stuck derivation/read retains the phase fail-closed. The job stops ten seconds before recovery expiry to reserve cleanup and post-attestation time; a separate reserve covers keyless verification. After recovery closes, a fresh keyless process verifies the exact payload and must exit before success. The process/job key allowlist and enrollment verifier context are extended narrowly; no dependency or renderer/IPC change is involved.
