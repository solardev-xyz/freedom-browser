**Fresh keyless verification:** [A separate utility](https://github.com/solardev-xyz/freedom-browser/blob/c2b5acc02cbf2d66220fcf847464a2e3b176b698/docs/railgun-poi-verifier-2026-10-04.md) verifies the canonical payload, binds its digest and the complete job input, rejects changed signals and returns only after observed exit. Checkpoint index/list metadata are not SNARK signals: changing the index still verifies but changes the payload digest. The enrollment context for this verifier remains unavailable until deliberate controller integration.
