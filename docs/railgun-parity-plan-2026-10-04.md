## Invariants after extraction

Preparation consent precedes input-specific POI/staging disclosure and signing; a later Ethereum review cannot authorize earlier queries retroactively. Staging consent, exact-root consent and membership consent remain distinct. Shield is submitted through its public lane, never the private broadcaster. Reading balances/notes is unverified observation and never spending authority.

Cancellation refuses new admissions while original admitted work drains. Receipt expiry, a copied result, restart or a changed source policy cannot mint another completion, discard a signed hold or authorize an automatic resend. Source-policy rotation may rebuild derived generations while the capsule, reservation, POI and EOA journal retain their authenticated interpretation.

The owner package and Freedom host must share genuine objects through the fixed composition. A second physical package copy, foreign receipt or uninitialized/preempted bootstrap must refuse before signing/journal admission. Ordinary EOA sends must not depend on initializing Railgun. Worker realms have their own bootstrap and module caches; do not pass main authority registries across IPC.

Evidence reports distinguish source/unit tests, native cryptography under synthetic services, packaged loading, and live external behavior. Neither test totals nor historical source hashes substitute for the missing live or cross-platform milestones.
