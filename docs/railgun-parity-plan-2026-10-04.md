1. **Qualify additional adopters and uncovered package paths.** Source extraction, pinned Freedom adoption, the five-case installed private preparation/restart campaign and unsigned packaged initialization are complete. Keep their exact source/runtime scope: the new retained-POI facade methods have source/unit coverage, and a second application still needs independent execution/storage host qualification. 2. **Complete live private qualification when a fresh bounded run is technically ready.** The latest [Tor continuation](railgun-tor-setup-deadlines-2026-10-07.md#final-live-continuation-at-c208245f) stopped at its deployment-anchor RPC with `TOR_REQUEST_FAILED`, after submitter EOA reads and POI membership but before its private-preflight nullifier, transaction review, journaled attempt or send. The held input remains reserved; that allowance is consumed. Do not reset it, infer a root cause from the generic error, or treat prior gas-estimation disclosure as proof nobody else submitted the calldata. No new live attempt is scheduled. The remaining sequence is private transfer, output POI submission, cold scan recovery, Valid status, and unshield/observation; an uncertain send permits observation only. Self-broadcast pays gas from a public EOA and does not qualify relayer privacy.
3. **Build the actual relay handoff separately.** External handoff needs the incompatible v5 attempt boundary, durable uncertainty, original-attempt reconciliation and confined authenticated transport before broadcaster use. Current v4 local records cannot silently become external attempts. Ten retained relay operations per account and no pruning remain limits. Broadcaster discovery/service availability, authentic POI/list behavior and Tor transport still need qualification; do not enable retries or fallbacks from synthetic success alone.
4. **Resolve remaining policy and product work.** Existing-only origin diagnostics are authenticated local evidence, not return-to-origin spending authority. Any origin policy must bind a finalized self-funded Shield, exact sender/recipient and unspent input while preserving ordinary proof/reservation/preflight gates and disclosing public linkage. No SDK first-transfer fallback or POI bypass is enabled. UI/UX, IPC activation, privacy explanations, platform/OS egress and circuit isolation, outage/performance coverage and production release review remain separate shared milestones.

## Invariants after extraction

Preparation consent precedes input-specific POI/staging disclosure and signing; a later Ethereum review cannot authorize earlier queries retroactively. Staging consent, exact-root consent and membership consent remain distinct. Shield is submitted through its public lane, never the private broadcaster. Reading balances/notes is unverified observation and never spending authority.

Cancellation refuses new admissions while original admitted work drains. Receipt expiry, a copied result, restart or a changed source policy cannot mint another completion, discard a signed hold or authorize an automatic resend. Source-policy rotation may rebuild derived generations while the capsule, reservation, POI and EOA journal retain their authenticated interpretation.

The owner package and Freedom host must share genuine objects through the fixed composition. A second physical package copy, foreign receipt or uninitialized/preempted bootstrap must refuse before signing/journal admission. Ordinary EOA sends must not depend on initializing Railgun. Worker realms have their own bootstrap and module caches; do not pass main authority registries across IPC.

Evidence reports distinguish source/unit tests, native cryptography under synthetic services, packaged loading, and live external behavior. Neither test totals nor historical source hashes substitute for the missing live or cross-platform milestones.
