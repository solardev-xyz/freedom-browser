Pages in this window only load through Tor, so this one opens once Tor is connected. If the Tor badge says “not connected”, enter the address again to retry.

That address can’t open in a Tor window

Swarm, IPFS, Radicle and on-chain addresses, and ENS or Tezos names, load through your own nodes and blockchain RPC providers, which don’t go through Tor. Open it in a normal or private window instead.

Pages in this window go through the Tor network: the sites you visit see a Tor address instead of yours, and your network provider sees that you use Tor, but not which sites. It’s also a private window: nothing is saved to history, and cookies and site data vanish when you close it.

What this window does

  • Sites see a Tor address instead of yours
  • Your network provider only sees that you use Tor, not which sites
  • Tor looks up the sites’ names, so they don’t leak to your DNS
  • WebRTC can’t reveal your IP address
  • Tor starts when the window opens; until it’s connected, pages wait rather than going direct

What it doesn’t do

  • It isn’t Tor Browser: sites can still recognise Freedom’s fingerprint across visits
  • Sites you sign in to still know it’s you
  • Swarm, IPFS, Radicle and ENS names don’t open here: they’d go outside Tor
  • Freedom’s own requests, like updates and your wallet, don’t use Tor
