Private window

You’re browsing privately

Tabs in this window leave no trace in this profile: nothing you visit here is saved to history, cookies and site data vanish when you close the window, and the address bar won’t learn from what you type.

What this window protects

  • No browsing or search history is saved on this device
  • Cookies, logins, and site data are discarded when the window closes
  • Downloads disappear from the downloads list (files you saved stay on disk)
  • Site permissions you grant here last only until the window closes

What it does not protect

  • Websites you sign in to still know it’s you
  • Your network provider, employer, or school can still see your traffic
  • Swarm, IPFS, and Radicle peers still see the requests your nodes make
  • Your IP address is still visible to every site and peer you connect to

Wallet is disabled in private windows. Your identity and wallet are persistent by design, so dApps opened here can’t see window.ethereum, window.swarm, or window.radicle, and pay-per-request (x402) content won’t unlock. Use a normal window for anything that needs your wallet or identity.
